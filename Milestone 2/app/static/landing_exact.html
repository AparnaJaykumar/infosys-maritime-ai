Frequently asked questions
What is FreightQuote AI?
A freight workspace where AI agents plan routes, compare carriers and price ocean freight quotes.
Do I need to enter payment details?
No. This is a demonstration project and no payment details are collected.
What is the difference between User and Admin accounts?
Users work with quotes and the Copilot. Admins can also manage registered users and unlock accounts.
How do I recover my password?
Answer your security question or request a six-digit code sent to your registered email.
